Our Mission

The CIS Club aims to foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous learning among students of Computing and Information Systems at DIU. We promote practical skills in programming, AI, networking, and emerging technologies. We provide a platform for students to showcase projects, collaborate, and solve real-world problems, while inspiring leadership, teamwork, and creativity.

History

The CIS Club was founded at Daffodil International University (DIU) to connect students passionate about computing and technology. Over the years, the club has organized coding competitions, hackathons, and workshops. We have partnered with industry professionals to provide mentorship and built a strong community of students dedicated to innovation and problem-solving.
